# **Addendum: Vector Vacuum Validation (The GPS)**

Subject: Physics of Trajectory & Risk:Reward  
Module Role: Optional Filter

## **1\. The Concept: Vector Vacuum**

While Field Theory tells us **where to enter** (Zone A), Vector Vacuum theory tells us **if there is room to run** to the target (Zone B).

* **Vacuum:** A price range with low structural resistance.  
* **GPS Logic:** We calculate the distance between the Entry Zone (Point A) and the next structural obstacle (Point B).

## **2\. Definitions**

* $V\_{vac}$ **(Vacuum Distance):** The pip distance between Entry and Target.  
* $R\_{risk}$ **(Zone Width):** The pip distance between Entry and Stop Loss.  
* $GPS\_{score}$ **(Projected R):** $V\_{vac} / R\_{risk}$.

## **3\. The Hypothesis**

*Trades where* $GPS\_{score} \< 2.0$ *(Compressed Vector) have a negative Expectancy regardless of the quality of the Entry Zone. Buying into a ceiling invalidates the Field.*

## **4\. Execution Logic**

1. **Identify Entry:** Zone A (H1 or Refined M15).  
2. **Identify Target:** Zone B (Next Swing High/Low or Opposing Zone).  
3. **Calculate:** $(Target \- Entry) / ZoneRisk$.  
4. **Filter:** If Result \< 2.0, tag as NO\_GO.
