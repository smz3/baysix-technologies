### **1\. The Core Research: Topological Data Analysis (TDA)**

You initially identified the work of **Marian Gidea and Yuri Katz** regarding "Topological Regime Fingerprinting." In academic literature, this is formally recognized as **Topological Data Analysis (TDA)** applied to financial time series.

* **Key Paper:** Gidea, M., & Katz, Y. A. (2017). *Topological Data Analysis of Financial Time Series: Landscapes of Crashes*. SSRN Electronic Journal. [https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.2931836](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.2931836)
